⏳ Bryan 實戰提醒：千萬別把轉車時間排太緊！ Bryan's Pro Tip: Don't schedule transfers too tight!

歐洲大眾運輸不像台日韓中那樣精準，有些國家特別容易誤點（比如臭名昭彰的德國鐵路 DB）。在安排「火車轉巴士」或「不同交通工具切換」時，務必預留極度充足的緩衝時間。

European public transport is not as punctual as in Taiwan, Japan, Korea, or China. Some countries are notorious for delays (like the infamous Deutsche Bahn - DB). When planning "train-to-bus" or "switching transport modes", make sure to leave plenty of buffer time.

⚠️ 隱藏陷阱：站點距離與迷宮轉運站 Hidden Trap: Station Distances and Maze-like Terminals

在大城市，長途巴士站通常設在城郊，如果你要巴士轉火車（或反過來），搭上市區公車可能還要快一小時才會到火車站。此外，你還需要時間在偌大的車站裡尋找「你的巴士到底停在哪個月台」。 In major cities, long-distance bus stations are often in the suburbs. If transferring from bus to train, taking a city bus to the train station might take nearly an hour. Plus, you need time to navigate large stations to find exactly which platform your bus departs from.

💀 個人血淚經歷： 💀 Personal Hard-Learned Experience: 在土耳其伊士麥（Izmir），長途巴士站不僅遠在城郊，而且就像「台北轉運站」一樣有好幾層樓、無數個月台！當時光是找我的車子停在哪個車位，就找得我暈頭轉向。如果轉車時間抓太緊，絕對會眼睜睜看著車跑掉！ In Izmir, Turkey, the bus terminal was not only far in the suburbs but had multiple floors and countless platforms! Just finding which bay my bus was parked in made me dizzy. If the transfer time is too tight, you will absolutely watch your bus leave without you!

常用廉航清單 Common Budget Airlines

  • Ryanair：全歐洲霸主，便宜但規定最嚴。Europe's giant, cheap but strictest rules.
  • easyJet：西歐為主，停靠較多主要機場。Focuses on Western Europe, stops at major airports.

  • Norwegian：北歐地區首選。Top choice for the Nordic region.

⚠️ 廉航防雷三大守則 ⚠️ 3 Golden Rules for Budget Airlines

  • 線上 Check-in：Online Check-in: 務必在起飛前 24h-2h 完成線上報到。若臨櫃辦理，可能會罰款高達 55 歐元！Must be done 24h-2h before departure. Airport check-in may incur a fine up to €55!
  • 免費小包體積：Free Bag Size: 出門前絕對不要塞太多，也建議用捲尺量一下尺寸，否則現場加購可能會高達60歐元！若到了機場仍不確定，一定要使用登機門旁的測量架。通常都是40 × 30 × 20 cm(需能塞進前方座位下)Don't overpack. Measure before leaving! On-site baggage fees can reach €60. Always use the sizers near boarding gates. Usually 40 × 30 × 20 cm (must fit under seat).
  • 機場位置：Airport Location: 廉航常飛「次要機場」(如巴黎 Beauvais 離市區 1.5 小時)，請先算好交通成本。LCCs often fly to "secondary airports" (e.g., Paris Beauvais is 1.5h away). Factor in transport costs.

🛡️ 歐洲機場安檢求生清單 European Airport Security Survival Checklist

🥛 水瓶：🥛 Water Bottles: 過安檢前倒空。安檢後有時有飲水機，或直接去廁所裝水(Tap Water)。Empty before security. Refill at fountains or restrooms (tap water is safe) after.

💧 液體：💧 Liquids: 單瓶不得超過 100ml，需裝入 1 公升透明夾鏈袋。Max 100ml per container, all in a 1-liter clear ziplock bag.

🧥 穿著：🧥 Clothing: 穿靴子、大外套或有皮帶，安檢時通常需脫下。建議穿方便穿脫的鞋。Boots, heavy coats, or belts must usually be removed. Wear slip-on shoes.

🔋 行動電源：🔋 Power Banks: 嚴禁托運NO CHECK-IN，必須隨身攜帶。額定容量需在 100Wh 以下、最多帶兩個、且飛機上不可使用。, must be carried on. Under 100Wh, max 2, and do not use on the plane.

💻 電子產品：💻 Electronics: 過安檢時，筆電、平板、大相機請單獨拿出來過 X 光機。Take laptops, tablets, and large cameras out separately for the X-ray.

✂️ 尖銳物：✂️ Sharp Objects: 刀刃 6cm 以下理論上可帶，但安檢人員有權沒收，建議貴重瑞士刀不要冒險。Blades under 6cm are theoretically allowed, but security may confiscate. Don't risk Swiss knives.

雖然 OTA (旅遊代購平台，如 Trip.com) 有時更便宜，但加購行李通常會被貴一波，或可能在班機有異常時較難去處理。這種情況強烈建議直接在航空公司官網買、或是選用較有公信力的OTA。 若小包超過一點點，趕快把衣服穿在身上或塞進大衣口袋！ 不過小確幸是，歐洲廉航是可以帶自己的食物上飛機吃的。而近期有些機場設備更新，能將電子產品留在包包裡、或是能直接帶水進去(如馬爾他機場)！ Although OTAs (like Trip.com) are sometimes cheaper, luggage add-ons are often overpriced, and handling flight disruptions is harder. We highly recommend booking directly on official airline sites or using highly reputable OTAs. If your small bag is slightly oversized, wear extra layers or stuff pockets! A small blessing: you can bring your own food onto European budget flights. Recently, some updated airports allow electronics in bags or even water through security (e.g., Malta Airport)!

火車：Eurail Pass 與各國國鐵 Trains: Eurail Pass & National Railways

1. Eurail Pass 歐洲火車通票 1. Eurail Pass

時機：When to use: 短時間內頻繁跨國移動。到官網或 App 購買後，開通手機動態電子票即可驗票（歐洲多為人工車上驗票）。 Frequent cross-border travel in a short period. Buy on the website/App, activate mobile e-tickets for on-board manual checks.

陷阱警告：Trap Warning: 有通票不代表無敵！高速鐵路（如法國 TGV、義大利紅箭、西班牙 AVE / AVANT、歐洲之星）必須額外付費強制劃位 (Seat Reservation)。熱門路段（如巴黎-米蘭）若不提早幾週劃位，即使有通票也上不了車。 A pass isn't invincible! High-speed trains (TGV, Frecciarossa, AVE, Eurostar) require mandatory paid Seat Reservations. For popular routes, if you don't book weeks ahead, you can't board even with a pass.

  • 價格：Price: €63 (2026年標準)。€63 (2026 standard).

3. 各國國鐵單程票 3. National Railway Single Tickets

購票原則：高速鐵路越早買越便宜Booking Principle: High-speed rail is cheaper the earlier you buy （早鳥票差距可達 3 倍）；區域鐵路票價固定，當天買即可。 (up to 3x cheaper); regional train prices are fixed, buy them on the day.

💡
跨國買票密技：Cross-border Ticketing Tip:
往返兩國的火車，一定要去「兩國的國鐵官網」分別查價！原則是東/南歐比西/北歐便宜。例如：維也納到布拉格的火車，從「捷克國鐵 (CD)」買，通常會比從「奧地利國鐵 (ÖBB)」買便宜非常多！ Always check BOTH countries' official railway websites! Rule of thumb: East/South Europe is cheaper than West/North. E.g., Vienna to Prague is usually much cheaper via "Czech Railways (CD)" than "Austrian Railways (ÖBB)"!

過夜火車 (Night Trains) Night Trains

既能省下一晚住宿費，又能完成長途移動，是背包客的最愛。目前歐洲夜車由奧地利國鐵的 ÖBB Nightjet 稱霸。 Saves a night of accommodation while completing long-distance travel; a backpacker's favorite. Currently dominated by Austria's ÖBB Nightjet.

  • 搶票時間：Booking Timing: 夜臥鋪非常搶手！強烈建議 3 到 6 個月前開放訂票時就去搶。Sleepers are extremely popular! Highly recommend booking 3-6 months in advance.
  • 艙等選擇：Cabin Choice: 千萬別為了省錢選座位 (Seats)，會生不如死。建議至少選擇 Couchette (4-6人硬臥室)。Never choose seats to save money, it's a nightmare. Recommend at least Couchette (4-6 person sleeper).
