為什麼創立這個網站？ Why did I create this website?

你是否也曾坐在火車站月台，看著因為臨時罷工(或甚至原因不明)而取消的班次，感到一陣恐慌？或者在擁擠的景點，下意識地死死抓著包包，擔心下一秒錢包就不見了？ Have you ever sat on a train station platform, feeling a wave of panic as your train is canceled due to a sudden strike (or even for unknown reasons)? Or unconsciously clutched your bag at a crowded attraction, worried your wallet might disappear the next second?

這些我都經歷過。我曾為了在瑞士省錢，連續一週半吃超市的麵包+煙燻火腿；也曾因為省錢沒注意評價，在剛重新裝潢的青旅與床蝨熱吻，留下來的深刻記號幾周不散。 I have experienced all of this. To save money in Switzerland, I once ate supermarket bread and smoked ham for a week and a half straight; to save money without checking reviews, I once passionately kissed bedbugs in a newly renovated hostel, leaving deep marks that lasted for weeks.

「獨旅不是因為沒有朋友，而是為了能更誠實地面對自己。」 "Solo travel isn't because you have no friends, but to face yourself more honestly."

在這個網站，我不分享修圖完美的網美照。我分享的是「真實的邏輯」：哪一條路線最省錢方便、哪一些景點要先訂票或最值得去、以及如何用最精簡的預算，換取最有質感的旅遊體驗。 On this website, I don't share perfectly photoshopped influencer photos. I share "true logic": which route is the cheapest and most convenient, which attractions need advance booking or are most worth visiting, and how to use the leanest budget to get the highest quality travel experience.
