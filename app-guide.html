💡 Bryan 的省錢攻略：下載 APP 前的斷捨離 Bryan's Strategy: Declutter Before Downloading

在開始介紹這份清單前，先破解一個常見迷思：「是不是所有服務都要下載 APP？」 Before diving into this list, let's bust a common myth: "Do I need to download an app for everything?"

其實不需要。像找機票的 Skyscanner，用網頁版就已經相當方便，沒必要額外佔用手機空間(除非你想放著作紀念><)。至於各國國鐵、區域交通或廉航的 APP，建議依照實際行程再決定是否下載。 Actually, no. For instance, finding flights on Skyscanner is perfectly fine via their website; there's no need to take up phone storage (unless you want it as a souvenir ><). As for national railways, regional transit, or budget airline apps, decide based on your actual itinerary.

🧠 更聰明的做法A Smarter Approach

「先規劃路線，再選工具」。建議先參考我的 歐洲交通指南 定好大框架，再決定手機該裝什麼。 "Plan the route first, pick tools later". I recommend checking my Europe Transit Guide to set your framework before deciding what apps to install.

⚖️ 判斷標準Criteria for Downloading

如果只是短暫停留、景點集中、或僅將城市作為中繼點，完全不需下載當地交通 APP；反之，若需頻繁搭乘大眾運輸才需安裝。 If you're only making a brief stop, attractions are clustered, or it's just a transit hub, you don't need the local transit app. Install them only if you will frequently use public transport.

街頭求生工具Street Survival Tools

🧠 Bryan 的生存哲學：隨時清空、補滿Bryan's Philosophy: Always Empty & Refill

在歐洲，免費廁所與裝水是「稀缺資源」。
我的核心心法是：只要有機會就使用，不要管當下是否急需。 In Europe, free toilets and water are "scarce resources".
My core rule: Use them whenever you see them, regardless of current need.

1. 移動站點：Transit Hubs: 火車、長途巴士與機場（除了極少數廉航機場）是最佳免費清理時間。Trains, long-distance buses, and airports are the best free toilet opportunities.

2. 百貨與速食：Malls & Fast Food: 認準精品百貨；車站與觀光區的速食店通常需憑單據條碼才能進廁所。Luxury malls are usually free. Fast food places in tourist areas require receipt barcodes to enter.

* 完整直飲水地圖與防雷細節，請見 安全與求生全攻略。For water maps and trap-dodging details, see the Full Safety Guide.

💡 Bryan 實戰經驗Bryan's Experience
