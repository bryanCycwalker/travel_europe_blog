特別注意：Special Notes:
  • 貨幣與換匯：使用福林 (HUF)。千萬不要在路邊換匯所換錢，匯率極差且常有隱藏手續費，強烈建議改用 ATM 直接提款或全程刷卡。 Currency: Uses Forint (HUF). Never exchange money at street kiosks; the rates are terrible with hidden fees. Use ATMs or cards instead.
  • 小費文化：在布達佩斯餐廳用餐，帳單通常會「自動計入」服務費（約 10-15%），結帳前請務必看清楚，以免重複給小費。 Tipping: Restaurants in Budapest usually include a 10-15% service charge in the bill. Check before paying to avoid double-tipping.
  • 打票與查票極嚴：布達佩斯查票員極多。若使用「紙本車票」，進站前或上車時必須放入打票機打票；特別注意若使用 BudapestGO「電子單程票」，上車時也必須拿手機掃描車門或打票機上那張極不起眼的小 QR Code 驗票（或 NFC 感應），直到畫面跳出動態圖示才算生效，否則就算買了票，查到一律視同逃票重罰！ Strict Ticket Inspections: Inspectors are everywhere. For paper tickets, you MUST stamp them in machines before boarding. For BudapestGO "e-tickets", you MUST scan the tiny QR code on the vehicle door or validator (or use NFC) until a dynamic animation appears. Failing to validate is considered fare evasion and heavily fined!

🎫 查票員就在你身邊Inspectors Are Everywhere

布達佩斯大眾運輸的查票頻率極高！購買實體單程票進地鐵站前，請務必找到**橘色打票機**完成打洞；若使用 Budapest GO App 的電子票，上車前必須掃描車門外的 QR Code 驗證，絕對不要抱有僥倖心態。 Ticket inspections are extremely frequent! For paper tickets, you MUST punch them in the **orange validation machines** before entering. For the Budapest GO App e-ticket, scan the QR code outside the door before boarding. Don't risk it.

💶 小心觀光街換匯陷阱Beware of Exchange Traps

瓦茨街（Váci utca）等觀光大道上有許多換匯所，匯率通常非常不合理。布達佩斯刷卡普及率極高，真的需要現金時，請找標示為銀行（如 OTP Bank、Erste）的正規 ATM 提領福林。 Exchange kiosks on tourist streets like Váci utca have terrible rates. Card payment is highly prevalent in Budapest. If you need cash, use official bank ATMs (like OTP Bank, Erste) to withdraw HUF.

🗺️ 必做體驗：布達佩斯 25 大絕美景點 🗺️ Must-Do: 25 Beautiful Spots in Budapest

以多瑙河為界，左岸是充滿歷史古蹟的「布達（Buda）」，右岸則是繁華熱鬧的「佩斯（Pest）」。以下為你盤點雙子城最經典的地標： Divided by the Danube, the left bank is the historic "Buda", and the right bank is the bustling "Pest". Here are the most classic landmarks of the twin cities:

公共建築Public Building

1. 匈牙利國會大廈 (Országház)1. Hungarian Parliament Building

多瑙河畔最耀眼的建築瑰寶，融合了新哥德式與文藝復興風格。內部裝飾極其奢華，並存放著匈牙利國王的皇冠。 The most dazzling architectural gem on the Danube, blending Neo-Gothic and Renaissance styles. The interior is extremely luxurious and houses the Holy Crown of Hungary.

2. 多瑙河岸的鞋子 (Cipők a Duna-parton)2. Shoes on the Danube Bank

紀念二戰期間被法西斯民兵殺害的猶太人，他們被要求脫下鞋子後在河畔遭到槍決，散落的鐵鞋雕塑靜靜訴說著歷史的悲痕。 A memorial to the Jews killed by fascist militiamen during WWII. They were ordered to take off their shoes before being shot at the edge of the water. The iron shoe sculptures quietly tell a tragic history.

💭 Bryan 親測：創意但沉重的河畔裝置藝術，希望這恐怖的歷史永遠不要重演。 💭 Bryan's take: Creative yet heavy riverside installation art. Hoping this terrifying history never repeats.

4. 安德拉什大街 (Andrássy út)4. Andrássy Avenue

被列為世界文化遺產的林蔭大道。地底運行著歐洲大陸最古老的 M1 千禧地鐵線，兩旁坐落著高級精品店與宏偉別墅。 A World Heritage-listed boulevard. Beneath it runs the M1 Millennium Underground, the oldest on continental Europe. Lined with boutiques and grand villas.

💭 Bryan 親測：從聖伊什特萬聖殿一路延伸到英雄廣場，兩旁林立著精緻高檔的新藝術建築，走在上面有種來到巴黎香榭麗舍大道的感覺！ 💭 Bryan's take: Stretching from St. Stephen's Basilica to Heroes' Square, flanked by exquisite Art Nouveau buildings. Walking here feels like the Champs-Élysées in Paris!

7. 菸草街會堂 (Dohány utcai Zsinagóga)7. Dohány Street Synagogue

歐洲最大、世界第二大的猶太會堂。結合了摩爾與浪漫主義風格，旁邊有著令人動容的「生命之樹」紀念碑。 The largest synagogue in Europe, second largest in the world. Blends Moorish and Romantic styles, with the moving "Tree of Life" memorial next to it.

9. 中央市場 (Nagycsarnok)9. Central Market Hall

布達佩斯最大最古老的室內市場，有著美麗的彩色磁磚屋頂。一樓販售生鮮與香料(如紅椒粉)，二樓則是紀念品與熟食區。 Budapest's largest and oldest indoor market with a beautiful colorful tiled roof. Ground floor sells fresh produce and spices (like paprika), upper floor has souvenirs and food stalls.

🍝 在地美食指南：匈牙利的舌尖狂歡 🍝 Local Cuisine: A Hungarian Feast

🍽️ 必吃美食Must-Eat Foods

紅椒粉是匈牙利料理的靈魂！這裡的燉菜與甜點不僅極具特色，更是撫慰旅人身心的絕佳Comfort Food： Paprika is the soul of Hungarian cuisine! The stews and desserts here are not only unique but also make the perfect comfort food:

🍲 匈牙利燉牛肉 (Goulash / Gulyás) 🍲 Goulash (Gulyás)

可以說是匈牙利的國菜。與常見的濃稠燉肉不同，傳統的 Goulash 更接近於「肉湯」，加入紅椒粉、牛肉塊、馬鈴薯和蔬菜，喝起來溫暖又香濃。 Hungary's national dish. Unlike thick stews, traditional Goulash is more like a meat soup with paprika, beef, potatoes, and veggies. Warm and savory.

🍗 紅椒燉雞 (Chicken Paprikash) 🍗 Chicken Paprikash

雞肉燉煮得軟嫩，搭配充滿紅椒香氣的濃郁酸奶油醬汁，通常會配上特有的麵疙瘩 (Nokedli) 一起食用，風味十足。 Tender chicken stewed in a rich paprika and sour cream sauce. Usually served with traditional dumpling noodles (Nokedli). Packed with flavor.

🐟 漁夫湯 (Fisherman's Soup / Halászlé) 🐟 Fisherman's Soup (Halászlé)

鮮美的傳統魚湯，通常使用淡水魚熬煮，並加入大量的匈牙利紅椒粉調味，味道極度濃郁，是非常地道的在地美食。 A hearty traditional soup made with freshwater fish and heavily spiced with paprika. Extremely rich in flavor, a true local delicacy.

🥘 匈牙利燉菜 (Pörkölt / Lecsó) 🥘 Hungarian Stews (Pörkölt / Lecsó)

Lecsó 是一種以甜椒、番茄和洋蔥為基底的燉菜（有時加入香腸）；Pörkölt 則是將肉類燉煮至軟爛入味，這兩者都是非常推薦的飽足餐點。 Lecsó is a vegetable stew based on peppers, tomatoes, and onions; Pörkölt is slow-cooked tender meat stew. Both are highly recommended filling meals.

🧄 炸麵包 (Lángos) 🧄 Lángos (Fried Dough)

布達佩斯街頭最具代表性的美食。油炸的扁平麵團外酥內軟，傳統吃法是抹上大量蒜泥醬，再鋪滿厚厚的酸奶油和起司。 The most iconic street food in Budapest. Deep-fried flat dough rubbed with garlic and topped generously with sour cream and grated cheese.

🪵 煙囪捲 (Chimney Cake / Kürtőskalács) 🪵 Chimney Cake (Kürtőskalács)

將麵團捲在木棍上炭火烘烤的甜點，外層焦香酥脆、內層鬆軟，裹上肉桂或核桃粉，是邊走邊吃的最佳甜點。 Dough wrapped around a wooden spit and roasted over charcoal. Crispy outside, soft inside, coated in cinnamon or walnuts. The best walking sweet.

🍰 多布什蛋糕 (Dobos Torta) 🍰 Dobos Torta

傳統匈牙利海綿蛋糕，內餡是滑順的巧克力奶油，最上層則是硬脆的焦糖片，口感層次極其分明。 A traditional Hungarian sponge cake layered with smooth chocolate buttercream, topped with a hard, crunchy caramel glaze for amazing texture.

🇭🇺 匈牙利獨旅生存避雷箱Hungary Survival Box

💶

貨幣：使用福林 (HUF)Currency: Forint (HUF)

匈牙利使用當地貨幣福林（HUF），並非歐元。千萬不要在路邊（特別是瓦茨街或機場）的換匯所換錢，匯率極差且常被扣高手續費。 Hungary uses the Forint (HUF), not the Euro. Never exchange money at street kiosks (especially Váci utca or the airport) due to terrible rates and hidden fees.

💡 實戰建議：Pro Tip:

布達佩斯刷卡極度普及。如需現金，請拿著海外提款卡找標示為「OTP Bank」或「Erste」等正規銀行 ATM 直接提領。 Card payment is extremely common. If you need cash, use an international debit card at official bank ATMs like "OTP Bank" or "Erste".

🚇

交通：單程車票必打卡Transit: Validate Tickets

布達佩斯的查票非常嚴格，跟德國很類似。無論紙本或電子單程票，進地鐵站或上公車時，紙本的話務必要找到機器「打洞印時間」、若是電子務必一上車就找「小到不行的QR code」，並確保網路暢通！沒有打卡的票一律視同逃票罰款！ Ticket checks are strict, like in Germany. For single tickets, if paper, you must punch it in the machine. If electronic, you must scan the tiny QR code immediately upon boarding with active internet! Unvalidated tickets result in heavy fines.

💡 實戰建議：Pro Tip:

下載 Budapest GO App 買電子票最省事！但記得上車前要在車門外掃描 QR code 才算開通。 Downloading Budapest GO App for e-tickets is easiest! But remember to scan the QR code outside the door before boarding.

🍽️

餐飲：小費通常已計入Dining: Service Included

在布達佩斯多數的餐廳用餐，帳單上通常會自動加上 10% - 15% 的服務費（Service Charge）。 In most Budapest restaurants, a 10% - 15% Service Charge is usually automatically added to your bill.

💡 實戰建議：Pro Tip:

結帳前請務必看一下帳單明細，如果有寫上服務費，就不需要再額外留下小費囉，避免當了冤大頭！ Always check your bill before paying. If a service charge is listed, there's no need to leave an extra tip, avoiding overpaying!
