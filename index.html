💡 歐洲的淡旺季怎麼分？💡 High vs Low Season in Europe?
台灣的淡季不等於歐洲的淡季！除了最擁擠的 暑假 (6-9月) 外，復活節 (4月) 與 聖誕跨年 (12月) 是當地人傾巢而出的「絕對連假旺季」，只要卡到這三段時間，沒提前買票絕對進不去！ Europe's seasons are different! Besides crowded Summer (Jun-Sep), Easter (Apr) and Christmas/NYE (Dec) are absolute peak holidays. If you travel then, advance booking is strictly required!

  • 🔋 體力與心力 (血條)：🔋 Physical & Mental Energy: 不要小看南歐拖行李的石板路與上下坡，以及防小偷帶來的「決策疲勞」。 Never underestimate dragging luggage on Southern Europe's cobblestones, nor the "decision fatigue" from guarding against pickpockets.

關於站長About the Author

Hi, 我是I'm Bryan 👋

熱愛造訪世界知名景點，也迷戀那些轉角才會出現的驚喜。有時與旅伴同行，但更多時候選擇一人輕裝上路，目前已走訪 41 個歐洲國家。

旅途中，我曾因班次臨時取消而極限改票，也曾為了在瑞士節省開支，幾乎只靠超市過活，卻依然吃得心滿意足。

創立 Solo Europe 41 的初衷，是希望將這些實戰經驗轉化為有溫度的獨旅指南。讓你在面對歐洲旅途中那些突如其來的變數時，不再手足無措，而是更從容地應對。
Passionate about visiting world-famous landmarks, yet deeply fascinated by surprises found just around the corner. While I sometimes travel with companions, I mostly choose to hit the road solo and light, having explored 41 European countries to date.

On the road, I've done extreme ticket changes due to last-minute cancellations, and practically lived off supermarkets to save money in Switzerland—yet still ate with immense satisfaction.

The original intent behind Solo Europe 41 is to transform these field experiences into a warm solo travel guide. It aims to help you face sudden variables in Europe without panic, handling them with calm and grace.
